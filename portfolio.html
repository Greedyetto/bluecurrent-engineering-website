Industrial Data & AI

Engineering insight.
More value from data.

Condition monitoring · Predictive maintenance · Operational analytics

Practical engineering experience, extended with industrial data analysis and AI.

Edwin has completed an AI bootcamp, adding formal training to his electrical, automation and drives background. This combination connects data analysis with an understanding of the equipment, controls and operating conditions behind the signals.

BlueCurrent can help assess the available data, develop analytical models and explore applications for maintenance and operational performance. The starting point is a clear engineering question and a check of whether the data can support it.

Condition monitoring

Analyse equipment trends, alarms and changes in operating behaviour to support investigation and maintenance decisions.

Predictive maintenance

Assess data quality and develop models to investigate anomalies and early signs of degradation. Model findings need validation against equipment condition and operating history before they inform maintenance decisions.

Operational analytics

Develop dashboards and analysis for equipment performance, energy consumption and maintenance planning, with results interpreted in their engineering context.
